How it works

Three steps, zero lab coat.

01

Clip it to the bar

The Movesense Flash snaps into a low-profile sleeve mount. Under 10 grams, no cables, pairs once over Bluetooth.

02

Lift like normal

The sensor's onboard IMU samples acceleration at 104 Hz. V-Lo integrates it into bar position and velocity in real time, rep by rep.

03

Autoregulate the set

When velocity loss crosses your threshold, V-Lo tells you to stop, drop weight, or push on — before the bar speed does it for you.

Training zones

Load by bar speed, not by feel.

V-Lo classifies every rep against published velocity-based training zones, calibrated per lift and per lifter over time.

Squat and bench thresholds shown; deadlift runs its own curve since concentric velocity at 1RM sits lower for the pull. V-Lo recalibrates zone boundaries from your own logged maxes rather than population averages.
